ParthTech is a technology brand built around innovation, live experiences, community and digital products. The identity turns that ambition into a visual system that feels modern, dependable and energetic.

Explore the identity ↓
01 / The idea

A brand built from connection.

The visual language combines modular geometry with rounded forms. The mark draws from the letters P and T while suggesting technology, connection and a system of parts working together.

02 / Identity

One mark.
Many contexts.

The symbol is deliberately simple: four rounded modules create a recognizable silhouette that can scale from a tiny interface icon to environmental signage.
