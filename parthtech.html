01 / The idea

A brand built from connection.

The visual language starts with a simple idea: technology brings people, products and experiences together. Rounded geometric forms create a flexible symbol that can live across a website, mobile interface, physical signage and brand communications.

02 / Identity

One mark.
Many contexts.

The ParthTech symbol combines the visual language of the letters P and T with a modular technology motif. Its rounded geometry gives the brand a friendly, accessible character.

03 / Construction

Designed on
a grid.

Consistent proportions and generous corner radii keep the symbol stable, friendly and easy to reproduce.
